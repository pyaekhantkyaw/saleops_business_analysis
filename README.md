# SaleOps: End-to-End Automotive Sales Analytics

##  Project Overview
SaleOps is an end-to-end business analytics project that demonstrates how raw transactional sales data can be transformed into actionable business insights using **SQL, Python, and Microsoft Power BI**.

This project follows the complete analytics workflow:
- Data extraction and business querying
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Interactive business intelligence dashboards

**Objective:** Identify revenue drivers, product performance, warehouse efficiency, customer purchasing behavior, and operational opportunities to support data-driven business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze sales performance over time  
- Identify top-performing product lines  
- Compare revenue against sales volume  
- Evaluate warehouse performance  
- Understand customer purchasing behavior  
- Analyze payment method preferences  
- Build executive dashboards for decision-making  
- Generate business recommendations supported by data

## 📊 Analytics Workflow

### 1. SQL Business Analysis
SQL was used to transform raw sales records into meaningful business information.

**Key techniques:**
- Common Table Expressions (CTEs)  
- Window Functions  
- Ranking Functions  
- Revenue calculations  
- Customer segmentation  
- Warehouse performance analysis  

**Business questions answered:**
- Which product lines generate the highest revenue?  
- Which warehouse contributes the most sales?  
- How does customer type influence purchasing behavior?  
- Which products balance demand and pricing power?  
- Which warehouse generates the highest revenue per unit sold?  

---

### 2. Python Exploratory Data Analysis
After extracting business insights using SQL, Python was used for deeper analytical exploration.

**Analysis includes:**
- Sales trends over time  
- Rolling 7-day moving averages  
- Revenue distribution  
- Product performance comparison  
- Warehouse contribution analysis  
- Client type analysis  
- Payment method analysis  
- Statistical visualizations  
- Business storytelling  

Python provides flexibility for exploratory analysis that goes beyond traditional dashboards.

---

### 3. Power BI Dashboard
Power BI transforms the analytical findings into an interactive dashboard suitable for business users and executives.

**Dashboard pages include:**
- Executive Summary  
- Total Net Revenue  
- Total Orders  
- Revenue Trend  
- Revenue by Product Line  
- Revenue by Payment Method  
- Product & Warehouse Performance  
- Quantity Sold  
- Warehouse Comparison  
- Revenue per Order  
- Customer & Payment Insights  
- Payment Distribution  
- Payment Fee Analysis
